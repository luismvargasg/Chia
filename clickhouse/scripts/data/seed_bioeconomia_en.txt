Bioeconomy: production, conversion and use of renewable biological resources to obtain energy, materials, chemicals and food. Lignocellulosic biomass, forest biomass and agro-industrial residues; biorefinery concepts; bioethanol, biodiesel, biogas and biofuels; anaerobic digestion; pyrolysis, gasification and thermochemical biomass conversion; catalytic conversion of biomass into platform chemicals; bioplastics, biopolymers and biodegradable polymers; cellulose and lignin valorization; industrial fermentation, enzymatic hydrolysis, biocatalysis and microbial metabolic engineering; microalgae and algal biofuels; bioenergy crops; biomass supply chains and life cycle assessment of bio-based products; agroforestry, aquaculture and bio-based agricultural inputs such as biofertilizers.
